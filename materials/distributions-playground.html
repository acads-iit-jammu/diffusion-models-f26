The Distribution Playground

One loop, walked with your own hands: see a distribution, draw samples from it, fit the distribution back from the samples, then sample from the fit.

The trap to try. Choose the mixture, draw 3000 samples, and press Fit: the fit is a single Normal (the best one, by maximum likelihood) — and it puts its peak exactly where the data is emptiest. The family you fit matters as much as the fitting. How to measure how wrong this fit is — that is KL divergence, next lecture.
