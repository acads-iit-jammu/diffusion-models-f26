Curriculum

What is taught in which week. The course itself is organised by concept — the numbered Lecture Notes in the sidebar, which read linearly and do not shift when a lecture does. This page is the timetable laid over them: each card gives a week’s dates and the notes it covers. Assessment dates are on Assignments.

Three kinds of material

Lecture Notes are the numbered spine and follow the lectures. Tutorials sit alongside a note and go deeper on one thing. Perspectives are optional reinterpretations — a transport reading of the subject, and the measure-theoretic foundations — and nothing in the lectures depends on them. Draft is material not yet settled.

Lectures meet Monday, Tuesday and Friday, 1:00–1:50 pm in 11AC4004.

W04 · Aug 17, 18, 21M1

Sampling, and the First Deep Generative Model

Generation is sampling, and sampling is hard. Metropolis–Hastings solves it when the target can be written down up to a constant — and cannot be pointed at images, because there is no such target to write. So learn the proposal instead: the GAN, its min-max objective, the Jensen–Shannon theorem behind it, and why it is so hard to train.
